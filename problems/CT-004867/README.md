# CT-004867 · Hom sheaves over the complement of a closed subscheme: for X a Noetherian scheme, F quasi-coherent, G coherent, I a quasi-coherent ideal sheaf with closed subscheme Z and U = X \ Z, the colimit over n of Hom_{O_X}(I^n G, F) is canonically isomorphic to Hom_{O_U}(G&#124;_U, F&#124;_U); in particular colim_n Hom_{O_X}(I^n, F) = Gamma(U, F).
